FINDING: std::unordered_map::find costs ~9 ns for n >= 24 (as expected
for O(1)) but rises to ~44 ns as n approaches 20 from below, then drops
discontinuously at n = 24. The effect is reproducible, survives a 20x
larger batch, and persists with the vector loop removed -- so it is not
warm-up, not frequency ramp, and not contamination from the scan.

It is therefore NOT safe to read a crossover point off this harness.
No crossover number from this program appears on any slide. The Week 6
deck cites only the bound that the module's own committed benchmark
supports: at n = 100 the hash already wins by ~20x, so the crossover
lies below 100. Finding it precisely is Lab 3.4's job, on the student's
own machine, with a harness they have interrogated the way this one was.
